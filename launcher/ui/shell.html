Build your modded firmware here. Load your official Pērkons firmware, choose the mod repos, tick the mods and voice engines you want, and press PATCH IT. Then download the new firmware file and copy it to the SD card.
Every mod and engine, explained. Learn how to use the mods on your Pērkons (controls, pages, presets), and browse what each one does to choose what to include in your next build. No firmware needed to read them.
How much your mods and engines can do at once. The Mod Matrix keeps your mods within the Pērkons's memory, but how much processor the engines use is up to you. See how much each engine uses, where to put the heavy ones, and how to tell when it's too much.
What has changed in the mods and engines. Every mod's and engine's recent changes, newest first. The ones that arrived since you last looked are marked NEW: to get them on your Pērkons, build your firmware again and install it.
When something isn't right. Slow start-up, crackle, a mod you can't tick, the app not connecting, and where to get help.
New mods, engines and features are added all the time. The app gets the latest when it starts, and while it stays open it checks again by itself about once an hour: this button turns yellow when it finds something. Click to check now, see what changed and update without quitting. It also tells you when a new version of this app is out, with a link to download it.
